Several concerns people have, such as muscle loss and nutrient depletion, are associated with markers we measure. Having that baseline means you and your clinician can keep an eye on the right things rather than guessing. It does not prevent side effects, but it removes some of the blind spots.

Do I have to be on GLP-1 already?

No. Many people test before they start, to begin informed. Others test while on a medication to establish a baseline they can revisit over time. Either works.
